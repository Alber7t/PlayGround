Play classic chess rules: take turns, protect your king, and checkmate to win.

Every capture changes the game. The capturing piece takes on the captured piece’s movement power. Your king always keeps its royal king-step and cannot be captured; as a royal piece it also gains the stolen power. Kings glow gold so you always know where the crown is.

Tap a piece to see its legal moves. Tap a glowing square to move. A pawn reaching the far rank becomes a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.
